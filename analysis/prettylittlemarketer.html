Hi, I’m Sophie!

Hi, I’m Sophie! I started Pretty Little Marketer in June 2020 whilst having a cry on my sofa. I was a second year student feeling lonely at the scary thought of life after graduation.

A handful of Google Searches to help ease my questions left me more overwhelmed than I’d begun, and I wished for a safe space to learn, be encouraged and feel confident as a curious marketer.

I grabbed my phone, whipped open Instagram to set up a sparkly new page called Pretty Little Marketer and now here we are; a thriving online community of 300,000, your lovely self included.
